Eyal Schneid

Eyal Schneid is a Fixer & Builder CEO for technology-led organizations at critical inflection points, combining enterprise-scale leadership, commercial transformation, and hands-on execution to challenge assumptions, simplify complexity, and build momentum.

He brings 25+ years of international leadership across enterprise software, SaaS, digital transformation, and regulated or complex B2B markets. His experience includes managing large global teams, leading business units with over $300M in P&L responsibility, and closing enterprise contracts of up to $750M.
